Hi, I'm Aritra. I build models that learn from little supervision and the fast, low-level systems they run on.

I'm a senior at IIT Kanpur (class of 2027), and I work on machine learning for language and vision. Right now I'm working on self-supervised models that understand Indian Sign Language from video and pose, and building an SLM-based grammar correction system for Bangla.

Outside of ML, I enjoy working on low-level systems. This summer at Quadeye Securities I built the wire side of a 10G Ethernet NIC on an FPGA, and it ended with a Pre-Placement Offer.
